Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. This policy covers this website and HOW.EXE tools, the internal tool the HOW.EXE team uses to manage its own social media accounts.

Who we are

HOW.EXE is a small educational video channel run by two people. Contact: how.exe271@gmail.com.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, trackers or forms, and collects no personal data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and secure its service, under its own privacy statement.

What HOW.EXE tools does

HOW.EXE tools signs in to our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook through each platform's official API, with permissions we grant ourselves. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

  • It reads the statistics of our own videos (views, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time, audience breakdowns the platform provides in aggregate) and of our own accounts (follower counts).
  • It reads the public comments people post on our own videos: the commenter's public display name or username, the comment text, its time and its like count.
  • It posts replies to those comments, as our account, only after a team member has reviewed and approved each reply.

It does not read private messages, does not access anyone else's account, and does not collect email addresses, phone numbers or any data beyond what is listed above.

How the data is used and kept

  • Only to understand how our videos perform and to answer comments.
  • Stored in a private repository accessible only to the HOW.EXE team. Sign-in tokens stay on the team members' own computers and are never shared.
  • Never sold, rented or shared with third parties, and never used for advertising.
  • Kept for as long as the channel exists, so we can compare our videos over time. Comments deleted on the platform are no longer updated.

Your choices

If you commented on one of our videos and want your comment removed from our records, email how.exe271@gmail.com with the video and the comment, and we will delete it within 30 days. You can also delete the comment on the platform itself.

Platform terms

Data from each platform is handled under that platform's terms and policies: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy, TikTok Terms of Service, Meta Privacy Policy. HOW.EXE tools' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date.
